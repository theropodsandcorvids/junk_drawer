industry, I’ve seen first hand the consequences of using outdated and ad hoc processes, which can cause havoc

potentially costing millions of dollars.
Similar to the oil and gas industry, the movie industry is currently shifting from pen, paper and spreadsheets to more
sophisticated software. Both industries also have substantial inertial resistance to changing their daily routines. As a
result, software intended for either industry needs to provide extra handholding for the user, especially early on.

Technology has always been there to help make our lives a little easier. The invention of the wheel meant no more
dragging around back breaking loads. Now with a simple push of a cart you can easily get things where you need
them to go. So if you only have to load a bunch of heavy rocks in a wheelbarrow to be done in one trip, then
would you still choose to carry them one by one?
There are many companies today who still rely on methods that are the modern day equivalent of carrying
rocks. But this doesn’t have to be. The reasons why companies have not adopted new technology can vary,
however often it is because they haven’t been exposed to how they can access and implement these emerging
technologies to benefit their organization.
I’ve experienced this first hand on several projects where I had to sell the client on trying a new process. Usually
there was initial hesitation because a change in routine invites the fear of the unknown. And although what they
had in place was cumbersome and inefficient, it still got the job done well enough in their eyes. One of the keys
to getting buy-in from the client is not only demonstrating that the new tool will be more efficient then what they
are currently using, but showing them it will open up new capabilities beyond what they thought possible.
Getting clients to understand the benefits of something new and unknown, requires excellent communication.
Having worked in an agency setting and in contract positions, I’m familiar with guiding clients through projects,
keeping them informed using presentations, proposals, marketing and sales material, along with more hands-on
demonstrations. It’s also critical to be able to synthesize complex topics into something simple to communicate.
But once you do this and can truly get them to understand the utility of a wheelbarrow, then they will never carry
