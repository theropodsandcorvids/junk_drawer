Asset 05: Federal Grant Data Management Plan (DMP)

Uniform Guidance 2 CFR 200.315 / OSTP Open-Access Compliance Blueprint

1. Expected Data Formats & Deliverables

Digital Research Data Matrices

  • Quantitative Student Performance Data: Course enrollment matrices, withdrawal velocities, and final letter grades stored as flat-file comma-separated values (.csv).
  • Qualitative Intervention Logs: Intrusive advising hours and tutoring tracking summaries compiled as machine-readable data sets (.json).
  • Instructional Training Modules: Laboratory safety curricula and project dissemination documents archived as searchable document formats (.pdf).
2. Metadata Vocabularies & Schema Standards

Machine-Readable Documentation Standards

  • Dublin Core Mapping: All master directories will match the ANSI/NISO Z39.85 vocabulary layout (incorporating static title, creator, issued date, and format data fields).
  • Dossier Self-Containment: Every database export requires an accompanying plain-text README.txt file cataloging missing cell variables and tracking definitions.
3. Privacy Controls & Intellectual Property Rights

FERPA Protection & Re-use Licensure

  • Anonymization Interlocks: Direct identification parameters, social security strings, and internal student Banner codes are programmatically scrubbed.
  • Cell Suppression Rules: Core datasets automatically suppress calculation groups containing small sizes (n < 5) or extreme boundary conditions to prevent deductive disclosure.
  • Open Access Mandate: Disseminated software models and curricular guides will be released internationally under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license.
4. Long-Term Archiving & Preservation Lifecycles

LoboVault Storage Configuration

  • Primary Repository Placement: Anonymized annual performance files will be logged in the UNM Digital Repository (LoboVault) with static, permanent Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs).
  • Statutory Preservation Constraints: In direct alignment with 2 CFR § 200.334, all raw datasets, progress report records, and audit files are locked for a minimum retention window of three (3) years following final financial report sign-off.
